# RAG-Powered Chat App on the Vienna Conventions of 1961, 1963, and 1969

This is an implementation of a Retrieval-Augmented Generation chat app where you can ask questions about the Vienna Conventions of 1961, 193, and 1969. These conventions are on Diplomatic Relations, Consular Relations, and on Law of Treaties, respectively. This RAG application was designed to be restricted to the aforementioned topics and context.

The inspiration for this was to help a loved one use this app to study for foreign officer examinations for her career in diplomacy. This would act as a supplement to her reading of diplomacy topics, in this case the Vienna Conventions.


This app uses Google Gemini 2.0 Flash as its base LLM, MongoDB Atlas as its vector database, and is housed on Streamlit. You may access the app here: https://vienna-conventions-chat-application.streamlit.app/
